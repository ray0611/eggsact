Why are my times different from other timers?

Water boils at a lower temperature the higher you are, so eggs take longer. Eggsact uses your iPhone's barometer to find your boiling point. If your iPhone has no barometer, tap the altitude chip at the top of the Boil tab and pick your altitude.

My eggs came out a little off.

Every stove and pot is different. After a boil, choose "Too runny" or "Overcooked" and Eggsact will adjust future times. You can reset this under the altitude chip › Personal calibration.

I'm not getting alerts.

Open Settings › Notifications › Eggsact and turn on Allow Notifications. For the Lock Screen countdown, also turn on Live Activities.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Eggsact does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Barometer (air pressure) readings are used only on your device to calculate the boiling point of water. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings, timers and calibration are saved only on your device.
  • Notifications and Live Activities are created on your device; no push server is involved.
  • The app makes no network requests.

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change. Contact: baiyanlabs@gmail.com.
